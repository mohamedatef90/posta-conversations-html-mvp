01 · Identity

Logo System

Two lockups — mark + wordmark (vertical) and mark only (icon). Use the mark alone at sizes below 48px.

Primary · Light background
Primary · Dark background
Mark only · Neutral
Mark only · Brand gradient
✓ Do — Clear space

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "P" letterform on all sides.

✓ Do — Approved backgrounds

Use on white, light gray, brand navy, or the brand gradient. Never on mid-gray.

✗ Don't — Recolor the mark

Never recolor, flatten, or replace the gradient in the ribbon mark. It is brand-critical.

✗ Don't — Distort or rotate

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, add drop shadow, or apply effects to the logo.

✗ Don't — Low contrast

Never place the full lockup on a busy photo or pattern without a solid backing.

✓ Do — Min size

Wordmark lockup: min 120px wide in digital. Mark only: min 24px. Below 24px use text.

Motion Principles

Hover each card to preview the easing curve. Motion should feel responsive, never decorative.

Use: Entrances, reveals

cubic-bezier(0.4,0,1,1)
Duration: 150–200ms
Use: Exits, dismissals

cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)
Duration: 300ms
Use: Transitions, panels
